00AboutMission & Roadmap

Why MMI exists

Most hands-on engineering education — wiring guides, project tutorials, competition prep — is written for students who already read technical English fluently. That leaves out a huge number of capable students across the Arab world before they've even started.

MMI is an independent, open-access platform that removes that barrier. Every blueprint is a complete build guide in both English and Arabic. Every student, regardless of where they're starting from, gets a clear path from "I don't know what a microcontroller is" to a real project worth submitting to a science fair.

We're not a company and we don't sell anything — everything on this site is free, and the board that runs it is entirely student-led.

01FoundationsStart Here

New to this? Start with the basics.

Before the Vault's build guides make sense, a few core ideas need to click. Here's the short version of each — no prior electronics or coding background assumed.

?

What is a microcontroller?

A microcontroller is a tiny, cheap computer built to do one job: read inputs (like a button or sensor) and control outputs (like a light or motor) based on code you write. It's the "brain" behind almost every hardware project in this Vault.

▢

Meet the Arduino Uno

The Arduino Uno is the most common beginner microcontroller board in the world — cheap, well-documented, and used in the vast majority of student engineering projects. You'll write code for it in the free Arduino IDE and upload it over a USB cable.

◉

What's a sensor?

A sensor converts something physical — distance, temperature, light, motion — into an electrical signal a microcontroller can read. Every blueprint in the Vault centers on wiring and reading from one or more sensors.

▤

What's a breadboard?

A breadboard is a reusable board for building circuits without soldering — you plug components and wires into it directly. It's how every build in this Vault is prototyped before anything is made permanent.

These four ideas are enough to start Blueprint BP-001 below. A full step-by-step foundations course — going deeper into each concept — is next on the roadmap.

05TeamOperational Board

Four seats. One mission.

These are founding leadership positions. Each board member owns a core function of MMI, has the autonomy to improve it, and works closely with the rest of the board to shape the organization's growth. As MMI expands, board members will recruit, mentor, and lead volunteers or interns within their area — building teams that multiply MMI's impact across the region.

SEAT 01

Research & Resources Lead

Owns MMI's educational resource ecosystem — researching opportunities and programs, maintaining the Regional Pipeline Index, and keeping every resource accurate and current.

SEAT 02

Arabic Technical Lead

MMI's final technical quality check — verifies engineering accuracy and technical Arabic terminology on every blueprint before it publishes.

SEAT 03

Marketing & Outreach Manager

Owns MMI's public presence — social media, partnerships, and outreach to schools and STEM communities across the region.

SEAT 04

Operations & Community Manager

Keeps MMI running behind the scenes — onboarding, volunteer coordination, event registrations, and project tracking.
