Join the Board

Five seats, real responsibility, and direct ownership over a specific part of MMI. This isn't a resume line — it's actual work with actual output attached to your name.

Before you apply: every seat here has a genuine deliverable attached — blueprints published, translations shipped, fairs indexed, sign-ups driven. This is a working board, not an honorary one. If that's what you want, keep reading.

01RolesThe Five Seats

What each seat actually does

Seat 01

Technical Layout Lead

Owns the visual and formatting standard for every blueprint that goes into the Vault — schematic diagrams, PDF layout consistency, the title-block system. You'll work directly from the BP-001–003 template and extend it as new blueprints get added.

Design / technical~3–5 hrs/weekFigma, Canva, or code-based design helpful
Seat 02

Academic & Strategy Coordinator

Drives the Playbook module forward — expanding the Regional Pipeline Index with verified fair data, researching and vetting program listings, and keeping the Standardized Integration Timeline realistic and current.

Research-heavy~3–5 hrs/weekStrong fact-checking instincts
Seats 03 & 04

Technical Editor & Translator (×2)

Localizes every blueprint and Playbook resource into precise technical Arabic — the core of what makes MMI different from every English-only maker resource out there. You'll review and refine the machine-assisted drafts already in the Vault, then translate new material as it's written.

Fluent technical Arabic~4–6 hrs/weekEngineering background helpful, not required
Seat 05

Marketing & Outreach Manager

Owns MMI's public presence — launching and running the Instagram, driving the digital distribution push into schools and STEM communities across the region, and leading sign-ups for the Horizon Challenge. If MMI is going to reach students who need it, this seat is how.

Content & community~4–6 hrs/weekInstagram/social fluency, regional student network a plus
